Facebook city: open Facebook Marketplace in your own browser, pick your city, and copy the word after /marketplace/ in the address bar, for example vancouver, toronto or nyc. Somewhere other than Canada or the US? Use Item Hunter International.

Stores

Each store is checked on its own clock, shown beside it, and several are checked at once. Speed scales all of them. Stores that refuse fast visitors, Amazon and Canada Computers above all, stay slow on purpose.

Amazon also sells used and open-box stock through Amazon Resale, often well under the new price. With this on, the cheapest such offer for a product appears as its own row, marked "Used". Turn it off to watch new-in-box prices only.

Alerts

An alert goes out when a listing beats an item's best price, drops to within 5% of it, or reaches the item's target price. At most three per store per check, on every channel below.

Sending email needs an account to send from. For Gmail: host smtp.gmail.com, port 587, username is your full address, and the password must be a 16-character app password (Google account, Security, 2-Step Verification, App passwords). Outlook: smtp.office365.com, port 587, also with an app password. The password is kept in data/state.json on this PC.

Install the free ntfy app (Android or iPhone), subscribe to the same topic, and alerts arrive as push notifications. Anyone who knows the topic name can read it, so use a random one.
