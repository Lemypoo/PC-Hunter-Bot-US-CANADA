What this is

Item Hunter is a personal price-watching tool that you run on your own computer. It is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.

Prices and stock

Every price, stock status and listing comes from a third-party store's public search page at the moment it was checked. Stores change prices constantly and their pages can be misread by software. Always confirm the price and availability on the store's own page before buying. The app does not buy anything for you.
