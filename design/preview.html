Item Hunter UI

Five artboards in a warm dark palette. This is a flat preview; the pan and zoom canvas needs Node.

Dark palette. Ink #141413 ground, panels #1d1d1b, text #faf9f5, clay #d97757 on primary actions with #e89070 on the hero prices, olive #9ab37a for drops. Poppins for the interface, Lora for prose.

One type scale: 14px for everything, 16px for names and headings, 24px for the one price that matters. The uniform body size is why the cards are two across instead of three.

States row: nothing tracked yet, and a pass where two stores blocked the scraper while one item found no in-stock match and another sits paused.
